# PRODUCT.md — PeakNET

## Register
product (app UI is the core), with a brand surface for the public landing page (`src/app/page.tsx`).

## Product purpose
PeakNET is a Turkish YKS (university entrance exam) study platform. It turns a year of grinding into visible momentum: topic mastery tracking, an AI tutor and question solver, timed mock exams, spaced-repetition flashcards, a mistakes notebook, Pomodoro focus, net→ranking tools, and gamification (XP, levels, streaks, daily quests, achievements). All curriculum content is MEB/ÖSYM-aligned and original; never embed third-party copyrighted questions.

## Users
Turkish high-schoolers, roughly 16–18, preparing for TYT/AYT/YDT across all tracks (Sayısal/MF, Eşit Ağırlık/TM, Sözel, Dil). They study long hours, often late at night on a phone, frequently offline. They are stressed, motivated in bursts, and respond to streaks, progress, and small wins. Turkish-language UI.

## Tone
Motivating, direct, warm. A disciplined training partner, not a cute mascot and not a corporate dashboard. Confident, never childish. Turkish copy: clear, short, second person ("sen").

## Brand idea
"Zirve" — the climb to your peak score. Momentum, altitude, focus. Progress should feel like ascent: every session moves you up the mountain.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (gray cards, indigo button, hero-metric tiles).
- Duolingo-style candy/mascot childishness.
- Education-blue + white clinical look.
- "AI tool" purple-on-black cliché.
- Identical icon+title+text card grids repeated down the page.

## Strategic principles
- Dark-first (night study) with a genuinely refined warm light mode for daytime.
- Make progress tactile and rewarding: numbers are tabular, streaks feel alive, completing things feels good.
- Calm enough for 3-hour sessions; energetic at the moments that matter (results, level-ups, quests).
- Mobile and tablet first; the app is a PWA and a Capacitor Android app.
- Information-dense without feeling like a cockpit; strong hierarchy does the work.
